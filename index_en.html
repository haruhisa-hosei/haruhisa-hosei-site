鳳聲晴久
HARUHISA HOSEI
Shinobue & Nohkan Player / Traditional Arts Producer
Graduated from Tokyo University of the Arts, Department of Traditional Japanese Music.
Studied under Taneo Wakayama and Haruo Hosei in the Hosei School.

Based on classical Japanese music such as Edo Bayashi, Kagura, and Nagauta, performing on stages both in Japan and abroad. Continues activities to connect the world of the flute to the next generation through collaborations with contemporary music and jazz, regional cultural heritage projects, and hands-on workshops.

Main Productions & Appearances:
- Chiba Prefecture 150th Anniversary Commemorative Performance: Koshiro Matsumoto Kabuki Dance Performance "Renjishi" (Appearance/Production)
- Traditional Arts Schola "Let's Enjoy Kabuki" Production (Lecture, Demonstration, Instrument Experience)
- Niigata City Request "Kakubeijishi" Hayashi Restoration Project (Planning/Instruction)
